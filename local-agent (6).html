Tracks 33 body joints many times a second, so a gesture like a wave is measured, not guessed. It uses a tiny model of a few MB that runs alongside the AI, and even without one. While it is on, gestures, not general motion, trigger reviews.

Smoothing only changes how the lines look. Gesture detection always uses the raw measurements.

Body tracking uses Google's MediaPipe. Video is processed on this device; MediaPipe may send anonymous usage metrics to Google while online.
