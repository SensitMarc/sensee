Posture monitoring — long session

Designed to run for a full work session next to a client at their desk. Tracks forward head posture, shoulder level, and torso lean at a low sampling rate — not full-speed rep tracking. Camera placement matters: a 3/4-angle view (not straight-on, not pure side profile) is the best practical compromise — pure side view hides one shoulder, straight-on under-reads forward head lean. These thresholds are configurable heuristics for spotting patterns over time, not clinical diagnostic cutoffs.
