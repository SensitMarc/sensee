How detection works

Self-calibrating — no setup needed. Tracks hip-driven movement (squats, deadlifts, lunges), arm-driven movement (curls, presses, raises — works seated too), or a repeating dance/movement pattern. It locks onto whichever body region shows a real repeating motion first, so stay in one mode of movement per session for the cleanest count. Dance or freeform movement may under-count if it isn't built from a clear repeating shape.
